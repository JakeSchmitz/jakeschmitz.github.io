Privacy Policy · Last updated: June 9, 2026

Godwit is a hands-free birding companion. We designed it to keep your data on your device. Godwit does not have user accounts, does not run analytics or advertising, and does not collect or transmit your personal data to us or to any third-party tracker.

The short version

  • All bird identification (audio) and voice logging (speech) happen on your device. Audio and speech are never uploaded.
  • Your sightings, sessions, and walk track are stored locally on your device.
  • Location is used on your device to tag sightings and map your walk; it is not sent to us.
  • The only network requests Godwit makes are to fetch public reference photos of birds, and any export/sharing you initiate.

What Godwit accesses, and why

Microphone. While a listening session is running, Godwit processes audio on-device to identify bird calls. Short clips of detected calls may be kept locally so you can replay them (“Hear”). Audio is not sent off the device.

Speech recognition (optional — “Voice logging”). If you enable voice logging, Godwit uses Apple’s on-device speech recognition to turn a spoken bird name into a logged sighting. Speech is recognized on the device and is not sent to Apple’s servers or to us.

Location (optional). With your permission, Godwit uses your location to tag sightings and draw your session route, and to suggest a nearby place name for an eBird-format export. Location data is stored locally and is not transmitted to us.

Bluetooth (optional). If you use compatible glasses, Godwit connects over Bluetooth to show bird IDs on the display. No personal data is collected through this connection.

Bird reference photos (third-party services)

To show a photo of an identified species, Godwit uses bundled images where possible. When a species isn’t bundled, Godwit requests a public photo from Wikimedia Commons and/or iNaturalist by sending the species name (not any personal information). As with any web request, those services receive your device’s IP address. Their use of that information is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Wikimedia Foundation: foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy
  • iNaturalist: inaturalist.org/pages/privacy

Exporting your data

Godwit can generate an eBird-format CSV of your sightings on your device. If you choose to share or upload that file (for example, to eBird), that action is initiated by you through the iOS share sheet; Godwit does not transmit it automatically, and we never receive a copy.

Data retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device. You can delete individual sessions in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its local data. We hold no copy to delete because we never receive it.

Children’s privacy

Godwit is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new “Last updated” date.
